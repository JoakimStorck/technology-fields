\section*{Highlights}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Technologies are fields with position, reach, and amplitude in priced task space
  \item A price gate separates technical exposure from economic incidence
  \item Run in its own era, each of two automation waves restores work but not pay
  \item New work binds at one price under both waves; the price of what they take differs
  \item New AI and robotics firms concentrate on the ring where the model seeds new work
\end{itemize}
